A Collective for Eye Science

Seeing the whole spectrum
of vision science.

Treeye unites eye scientists and software engineers to build an open platform of documentation, tools, and applications for optometry & ophthalmology — from data to decisions.

01The Platform
Collect

Data Input

Bring eye data of every kind into one place.

  • Eye scan images — all modalities
  • Doctor & clinic records
  • Patient clinical records
  • Scientific articles & literature
Understand

Processing & Modeling

Turn raw data into knowledge.

  • Data processing pipelines
  • Modeling of eye & vision
  • Statistics & analysis
Decide

Decision-Support Tools

Help clinicians make the best call.

  • Prescriptions
  • Surgeries
  • Lens selection & more
